About us

The project and the people behind TIB Chat.

DigiStrucMed

Clinical guidelines hold the best available evidence on how to treat heart disease, but it is locked in long PDFs written for specialists. DigiStrucMed makes that knowledge accessible to patients, with every answer traceable to the guideline it comes from.

We turned 204 guideline documents, 2,780 pages, into structured knowledge: tables reconstructed row by row, 3,977 recommendations with their class of recommendation and level of evidence, linked to 8,882 medical concepts and mapped to UMLS and Wikidata. On top of that we built three agents that use the knowledge in different ways, following current research instead of ad-hoc prompting:

  • Retrieval over every guideline page, with answers that cite the page and quotes checked word for word against the source.
  • Fine-tuning of a small open model with neuro-symbolic chain-of-thought rules mined from the knowledge graph.
  • Graph reasoning with Think-on-Graph and HippoRAG-style personalized PageRank, shown live as the agent walks the graph.

A research demo for comparing answer quality, traceability and explainability. It does not give medical advice.
